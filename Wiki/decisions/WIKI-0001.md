# Separate Wiki from Study

## Context

The existing `cs` Obsidian Vault already holds authored learning materials under `Study/` and other folders. The intended LLM Wiki serves as durable context for AI clients, not a replacement learning notebook.

## Decision

Create `Wiki/` independently inside the same repository. Keep `Study/`, `Project/`, and other original directories unchanged. Prefer links to existing materials rather than duplicate copies.

## Rejected alternatives

- Rebuild all existing study notes as Wiki records: unnecessary duplication and risk of modifying original notes.
- Mix AI decision logs into every learning note: makes retrieval and ownership unclear.

## Consequences and constraints

- New Wiki content must be discoverable from `Wiki/index.md`.
- AI-oriented records may be terse, but decisions must retain rationale and provenance.
- Study content may be referenced without being automatically edited.

## Implementation/verification

Phase 1 scaffolding is proposed on a review branch. No claim is made that it is merged or synchronized to local devices.
