# Neutral, content-first planner UI

## Context
Repeated rounded cards, charcoal/sage tint and decorative surfaces made the planner feel artificial and reduced task hierarchy clarity.

## Decision
Use neutral grayscale, flat content sections, restrained hairline dividers, measured typography and compact controls. Preserve functional boundaries for dialogs, calendar cells and inputs, plus accessible focus states and user-authored colors.

## Rejected alternatives
- Repeated nested tiles and rounded cards.
- Decorative branding tints as core dark theme.

## Consequences and constraints
A visual refactor must not rewrite stored Todo/memo records or sacrifice accessible sizes and keyboard behavior. Compact does not mean uniformly shrinking everything.

## Implementation/verification
[PR #118](https://github.com/guseoh/dark-todo-planner/pull/118) merged 2026-10-30. [design.md](https://github.com/guseoh/dark-todo-planner/blob/main/design.md) defines the current UI contract.
