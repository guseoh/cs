# Learn architecture fundamentals through an example-led syllabus before independent system design

## Context

The learner wants to improve Java/Spring backend architecture and design judgment but considers immediate independent real-world system-design exercises premature. A publicly listed architecture course provides a progression from foundational concepts to quality, principles, patterns, documentation/evaluation, real-world cases, operations and architect growth.

## Decision

Use the **publicly listed 8-section, 59-topic curriculum** of [AI 시대, 개발자여 아키텍트로 오라!](https://www.inflearn.com/course/in-the-age-of-ai-dev?cid=343703) as an ordered topic scaffold for **independent learning with AI**, not for paid course attendance or verbatim lecture reproduction.

Teach through problem context, technical explanation, **concrete Java/Spring examples and execution flows**, diagrammatic comparisons, alternatives and trade-offs, and rigorously reviewed **Korean-language technical blogs and Korean-company engineering articles**. A reference link is supplemental: explain enough source-grounded substance in the lesson for the learner to understand the topic without opening the link.

Early lessons are instructor-led and explanation-first. Later, progressively analyze existing architecture and documented decisions (e.g. PawCycle with verified repo evidence) before asking the learner to make independent design decisions. Do not require mandatory quizzes, repeated gates, or fully independent designs before the learner is ready.

The detailed adaptation and per-topic examples are a **proposed study outline**, not proof that 59 lessons were completed or individually approved. It may be refined without changing the user-confirmed fundamental learning direction.

## Rejected alternatives

- Begin directly with high-pressure reservation/inventory/concurrency system design exercises.
- Replace the source curriculum with an unrelated or reordered abbreviated custom curriculum.
- Memorize fashionable patterns or architecture diagrams without examining failure modes, constraints and trade-offs.
- Rely solely on external links or purely abstract definitions without substantive worked examples.
- Treat a paid course as a required subscription, imply access to its nonpublic lessons, or copy proprietary lectures.

## Consequences and constraints

- Maintain the original course's eight-section progression; explain advanced ideas at a suitable depth using Java 21/Spring Boot, databases and operations where helpful.
- Distinguish public curriculum titles, independent technical explanation, company-specific case evidence, assumptions and unresolved claims.
- The existing [two-volume system-design-books study](BLR-0004.md), [separate AI-assisted domain design](BLR-0005.md) and [Large-scale Board practice roadmap](BLR-0003.md) continue as separate tracks. This decision does not authorize repo refactors or deployment.
- Existing daily design-scenario automation has **not** been changed by this record.

## Implementation/verification

On 2026-11-06 the course's public curriculum was inspected in an available browser tab and the user requested this curriculum-centered study roadmap. No individual lecture was watched or reproduced, no exercise outcome or learner proficiency was verified, and no source-project code was changed.
