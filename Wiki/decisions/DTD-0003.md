# Today drag order and compact Calendar details

## Context
Today task order could not be dragged; menus required repeated clicks, and the Calendar right-side detail panel was crowded.

## Decision
Use persisted drag ordering for incomplete Today tasks via existing reorder API, retaining order across All/category filters. Dismiss menus on outside click/Escape and after actions. Show compact date rows and category completion in the Calendar rail.

## Rejected alternatives
- Reorder only the filtered subset and disturb the ordering of other tasks.
- Keep the dense Calendar rail without clarifying individual tasks.

## Consequences and constraints
Keep existing data and keyboard behavior stable. Verify reload persistence and category-switch ordering separately.

## Implementation/verification
[PR #141](https://github.com/guseoh/dark-todo-planner/pull/141) merged 2026-11-02; [main CI](https://github.com/guseoh/dark-todo-planner/actions/runs/41377386788) passed including deployment. Independent authenticated production browser verification not performed here.
